> ⚠️ **HIPAA & Healthcare Compliance Notice:**
> Meta does not execute HIPAA Business Associate Agreements (BAAs). Under US Federal Trade Commission (FTC) enforcement and HHS OCR regulations (45 CFR § 164.502), installing commercial ad-tracking pixels on telehealth, therapy, or medical appointment pages without a BAA constitutes an unlawful disclosure of Protected Health Information (PHI). If you collect sensitive medical data or have Cal.com's BAA enabled, ad trackers are automatically suppressed on protected bookings.
